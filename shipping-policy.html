1. Order processing

All orders ship from our location in Athens, Alabama. Processing is the time we need to confirm your order, check payment, inspect your items and pack them, before the carrier picks them up.

  • Leather jackets: 1 to 3 business days.
  • Leather bag bundles (10 or 20 pieces): 3 to 5 business days. Every bag in a bundle is counted, inspected and packed by hand.
  • Large or custom orders: we confirm the ship date by email when we send your quote.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays. Orders confirmed after 2:00 PM Central Time, or on a weekend or holiday, start processing the next business day. Processing begins once your payment has cleared, or once we have agreed in writing to a payment arrangement.

Processing time and shipping time are separate. Your total delivery time is processing time plus the transit time of the shipping method you choose.

3. Shipping costs

Shipping is calculated from the weight and size of your package, the destination ZIP or postal code and the service you choose. We email you the exact shipping cost with your order confirmation, before you pay. There are no hidden handling fees.

  • Several items in one order ship together where possible, which lowers the cost per item.
  • Bag bundles ship in one or more cartons, and the cost reflects the total weight.
  • If we run a free or discounted shipping offer, the terms of that offer will be stated with it.
  • Sales tax is added where required by law and is shown on your confirmation.

4. Shipping within the United States

We ship to all 50 states and the District of Columbia.

  • Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories: allow 1 to 4 extra business days. Rates are higher and some express services are not available.
  • PO Boxes: we ship to PO Boxes by USPS only. UPS and FedEx cannot deliver to a PO Box.
  • Military addresses (APO, FPO, DPO): shipped by USPS only. Delivery usually takes 7 to 21 days.

5. International shipping

We ship worldwide with DHL Express, UPS and FedEx, except to countries or parties that U.S. export and sanctions laws prohibit us from serving.

  • The recipient is the importer of record. Customs duties, import taxes, VAT and brokerage fees are charged by the destination country and are not included in our prices or shipping cost.
  • Customs delays are outside our control. Please check your country's rules for importing leather goods before you order.
  • If a package is refused, or not collected because of unpaid duties, it is returned to us. We refund the item price minus our original shipping cost and the return shipping cost.
  • We declare the true value of every shipment. We cannot mark packages as gifts or understate their value.

6. Order tracking

When your order ships, we email you the carrier name and tracking number, usually within one business day of the carrier pickup. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first scan. You can follow your package on the carrier's website at any time.

7. Delivery, signatures and addresses

  • Signature: for orders of $500 or more, and for all international orders, we may require a signature on delivery. Packages without a signature requirement may be left at the delivery address.
  • Address accuracy: please check your shipping address carefully. We cannot be responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address you provided. If a package is returned to us because of a wrong or incomplete address, reshipping costs are paid by the customer.
  • Address changes: email us as soon as possible. We can change the address only if your order has not yet been handed to the carrier.
  • Safe delivery: if you are often away, choose a signature option or a secure delivery address.

8. Delays and our shipping promise

We ship your order within the time we state when you order. If we do not state a time, we ship within 30 days, as required by the Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule.

If we cannot meet the date, we email you before it passes, give you a new ship date, and let you either agree to the delay or cancel for a full refund.

Weather, carrier disruptions, customs, strikes and the holiday peak season (November and December) can slow delivery after your package leaves us. These delays are outside our control, but we will help you follow up with the carrier.

9. Lost, damaged or incorrect packages

  • Damaged in transit: check the package when it arrives. If it is damaged, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the box and the item. Keep all packaging.
  • Lost or stalled package: if tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact us. We open a trace with the carrier and, if it cannot be found, reship or refund your order.
  • Wrong or missing item: contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will correct it at no cost to you.

See our Refund & Return Policy for how replacements and refunds are handled.
