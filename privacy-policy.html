1. Who we are and what this covers

Nexvollt (“Nexvollt,” “we,” “us”) sells genuine leather bags and jackets from 110 Thomas St North, Apt 109 West Bldg, Athens, Alabama 35611. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit this website, place an order, contact us or join our email list. It is written for customers in the United States.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address and phone number.
  • Order details: shipping and billing address, the items you order, order notes and your payment method choice.
  • Messages: what you write in our contact form or in emails to us.
  • Email sign-up: your email address when you subscribe to updates.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, pages viewed, time on page, referring page and IP address (which gives us an approximate location).
  • Your shopping cart, which is saved in your own browser's local storage so it is still there when you return. It stays on your device.

Payment information

Card payments are taken through a third-party payment processor using a secure payment link. We do not see or store your full card number, expiration date or security code. We receive only confirmation that payment was made and, where available, the last four digits and card brand.

Information from others

Payment processors, shipping carriers and address verification services may give us information needed to complete and deliver your order or to prevent fraud.

3. How we use your information

  • To confirm, process, ship and support your order, including returns and refunds.
  • To send order, shipping and account messages (these are not marketing).
  • To send news about new styles and bundle offers, if you subscribed or where the law allows. You can opt out at any time.
  • To answer questions and improve our products, website and service.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To keep records and comply with tax, accounting and other legal duties.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed to run the store:

  • Payment processors to take and refund payments.
  • Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL) to deliver your order and send tracking updates.
  • Service providers that host our website, send our emails or help us operate, under terms that limit how they may use your data.
  • Authorities and advisers when required by law, subpoena or to protect rights, safety and property.
  • A buyer or successor if Nexvollt is sold or reorganized, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies, local storage and opt-out signals

Our site uses your browser's local storage to keep your cart. If we add cookies or analytics tools, we will describe them here. You can clear local storage or block cookies in your browser settings, but your cart may not be saved.

Where state law requires, we treat a browser's Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out of sale and sharing. Because there is no common standard for “Do Not Track” signals, we do not respond to them.

6. Marketing emails

We follow the federal CAN-SPAM Act. Marketing emails show our business name and address and include a clear unsubscribe option. We act on opt-out requests within 10 business days. You will still get order and shipping emails.

7. How long we keep information

We keep order records for up to 7 years for tax, accounting and legal reasons. Other information, such as contact messages and email list data, is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or until you ask us to delete it where the law allows.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and a third-party payment processor. No system is completely secure. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by Alabama law and other applicable state laws.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights. Many states, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon, give residents similar rights.

  • Know and access what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your personal information, with some exceptions such as records we must keep by law.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share it, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive information beyond what is needed to process your order.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

To make a request, email contact@nexvollt.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line, or call +1 256 875 7506. We may ask for details to verify your identity. We respond within 45 days, and we may extend this once by another 45 days if we tell you why. You can use an authorized agent, and we may ask for proof of their authority. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response, and you may contact your state attorney general.

10. Children

Our website is for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party sites and international visitors

Our site may link to other websites, such as carrier tracking pages. We do not control them, so please read their privacy policies. We are based in the United States, and information you give us is stored and processed here. If you visit from another country, you understand your information is transferred to the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. If we make a material change, we will post a notice on this site.
