Questions, answered.

What does “bundle” mean?

You buy the full bundle for the price shown. The FITTED Iconic Jacket is $400 for a bundle of 10, and the Urban Crossbody Bag is $250 for a bundle of 10. The Black Duo Crossbody Clutch comes in a bundle of 20.

What are the products made from?

Our bags are genuine cow leather, including full-grain milled leather. Our sheep leather and lambskin jackets are genuine leather. The FITTED jackets are premium faux leather. Every product page states the material.

Can I buy a single bag or a single Iconic Jacket?

Bags and the FITTED Iconic Jacket are listed as bundles. Contact us if you need a smaller quantity and we will tell you what is possible.

How do I care for a faux leather jacket?

Wipe it clean with a soft, damp cloth. Do not machine wash, bleach or tumble dry it, and keep it away from direct heat and prolonged sunlight to protect the finish.

Why does the Boxy Faux Leather Jacket say “Price on request”?

We have not published a price for it yet. Send us a message with the quantity you need and we will reply by email with a quote.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from Athens, Alabama within 1 to 3 business days for jackets and 3 to 5 for bundles, then take 1 to 7 business days in the U.S. depending on the method. See the full Shipping Policy for carriers, times and international orders.

Can you make custom orders?

Ask us. Tell us what you need in the contact form and we will reply by email.

What is your return policy?

Jackets can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unworn. Bag bundles can be returned within 14 days if unused and complete. Read the full Refund & Return Policy.
