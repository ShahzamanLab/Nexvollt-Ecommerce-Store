1. Returning a leather jacket

We want you to love your jacket. If it is not right, you can return it within 30 days of the delivery date for a refund or exchange, as long as it is:

  • unworn, unwashed and unaltered (trying it on indoors is fine);
  • free of perfume, smoke, pet hair, stains and odors;
  • returned with all tags, liners, removable parts (such as a fur collar) and the original packaging.

2. Returning a bag bundle

Bags are sold in bundles of 10 or 20 mainly to shops and resellers, so bundle returns work differently.

  • Notify us within 14 days of delivery.
  • The complete bundle must come back unused, in its original packaging, with every piece and accessory (straps, dust bags, tags).
  • A restocking fee of 15% of the bundle price applies to returns that are not caused by a defect or by our error.
  • We do not accept partial returns of a bundle, unless the pieces are defective or damaged (see below).

3. Items we cannot take back

  • Custom, personalized or made-to-order items.
  • Items marked final sale at the time of purchase.
  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered, stained, scented or damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned after the return window, or without our approval.

4. Defective, damaged or wrong items

If something arrives broken, defective or not what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We then:

  • send you a prepaid return label if we need the item back;
  • offer a replacement, or a full refund including the original shipping cost, whichever you prefer.

Leather is a natural material. Grain variation, small healed marks, slight color differences between pieces, creases and a natural leather smell are normal characteristics, not defects.

5. How to start a return

  1. Contact us. Email contact@nexvollt.com with your order number, the item and the reason for the return.
  2. Get approval. We reply within 2 business days with a return authorization number and the return address.
  3. Pack it well. Use the original packaging if you can, and put the authorization number inside the box.
  4. Ship it with tracking. Keep your receipt and tracking number until your refund arrives.

Please do not send items back without an authorization. Unapproved returns may be delayed or refused.

6. Return shipping costs

  • If you return an item because you changed your mind or need a different size, you pay the return shipping, and original shipping charges are not refunded.
  • If the return is due to a defect, damage or our mistake, we pay the return shipping.
  • We recommend a tracked and insured service. We cannot be responsible for returns that are lost or damaged on the way back to us.

7. Inspection and refunds

When your return arrives, we inspect it within 3 to 5 business days and email you the result.

  • Approved: we refund the item price (and sales tax on it) to your original payment method. Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days of approval. Your card issuer or bank may need another 3 to 10 days to show it.
  • Bank transfer orders: refunded by bank transfer. Any fees charged by your own bank are not covered.
  • Not approved: if a return does not meet this policy, we tell you why and can send it back at your cost.

If you have not received a refund after 15 business days, check with your bank first, then contact us.

8. Exchanges

You can exchange a jacket for another size within the 30-day window, subject to availability. Email us the size you need. If the new item costs more or less, we collect or refund the difference. We usually ship the exchange as soon as your return is received.

9. Cancelling an order

Email us as soon as you can. If your order has not yet shipped, we cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has shipped, the return process above applies.

10. Payment disputes

If you see a charge you do not understand, please contact us before you open a dispute with your card issuer. Most problems are fixed within one business day. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under federal or state law.
