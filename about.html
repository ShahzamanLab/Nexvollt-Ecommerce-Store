About us.

Who we are and how an order works.

Leather and faux leather, from Alabama.

Nexvollt sells leather bags and jackets to shoppers and to shop owners. Our bags come in bundles of 10 or 20, and our jackets include sheep leather and lambskin.

The FITTED collection adds jackets in premium faux leather, including the Iconic Jacket in a bundle of 10.

Every piece is checked before it ships. If you need something specific, ask us.
